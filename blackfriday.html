Black Friday shopping tips

  • Make a list now so you don't impulse-buy on the day.
  • Compare the exact same item across stores. Prices can differ a lot.
  • Order early from Temu and AliExpress; shipping can take 1–3 weeks.
  • Check return policies before you buy gifts.
  • Watch Cyber Monday (Nov 30) for extra online deals on tech and fashion.

❓ Black Friday FAQ

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday 2026 is on Friday, November 27, 2026, the day after Thanksgiving (Thursday, November 26). Cyber Monday is Monday, November 30, 2026.

Is Black Friday cheaper online or in store?

Many of the same deals are online, and you can compare prices across stores in seconds without lines. Some doorbusters are store-only, so check both if you want a specific item.

Which store has the best Black Friday deals?

It depends on what you buy. Amazon and Walmart are strong on tech, toys and home; Temu and AliExpress are cheapest for small gifts and decor; SHEIN is great for fashion. The smartest move is to compare the same item across stores before you buy.

When should I start Black Friday shopping?

Many stores start early deals in November, and AliExpress has its 11.11 sale on November 11. Make your list now, compare prices early, and order from slower-shipping stores like Temu and AliExpress at least 2–3 weeks before you need them.

What's the difference between Black Friday and Cyber Monday?

Black Friday (November 27) is famous for big doorbusters on TVs, toys and home. Cyber Monday (November 30) is online-focused and often has strong tech and fashion deals. Many sales run all weekend.
